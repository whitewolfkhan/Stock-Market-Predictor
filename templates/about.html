About the Stock Market Predictor

How to Use

This application uses a machine learning model to predict stock price trends.

  1. Stock Ticker: Enter the official stock ticker symbol for the company you are interested in (e.g., AAPL for Apple, GOOGL for Google).
  2. Start Date: Enter the beginning of the historical data range in YYYY-MM-DD format.
  3. End Date: Enter the end of the historical data range in YYYY-MM-DD format.
  4. Click the Predict button.

Understanding the Output

  • Chart: The chart displays the actual closing prices in blue and the model's predicted prices in orange.
  • Mean Squared Error (MSE): A measure of the average squared difference between the actual and predicted values. A lower MSE indicates a better fit.
  • R-squared (R2): Indicates how well the model's predictions approximate the actual values. A value of 1.0 is a perfect fit.
  • Next Day's Predicted Price: The model's forecast for the next trading day's closing price based on the most recent data.
